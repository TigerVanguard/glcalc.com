Calculate glycemic load from GI and serving size

Glycemic load is calculated as glycemic index multiplied by the grams of available carbohydrates in your serving, divided by 100. Use the interactive app to search foods, check GI values, convert serving sizes, and compare low, medium, and high glycemic load estimates.
